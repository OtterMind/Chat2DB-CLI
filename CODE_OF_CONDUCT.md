# Code of Conduct

## Our Community

Chat2DB CLI is an open-source project for developers, DBAs, analysts, and agent
tool builders. We want the community to be welcoming, respectful, and useful
for people with different backgrounds, experience levels, languages, and
workflows.

This Code of Conduct applies to Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, community
channels, and other public or private spaces connected to this project.

## Expected Behavior

We ask community members to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and receive feedback constructively.
- Stay focused on the project and the topic being discussed.
- Make Issues and Pull Requests clear and actionable.
- Respect different experience levels and communication styles.
- Protect other people's privacy and confidential information.
- Be open to correction and help resolve disagreements professionally.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or sexualized language, imagery, or attention.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or repeated disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing passwords, tokens, private data, or customer information.
- Any other behavior that maintainers reasonably consider inappropriate for
  the project community.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the official
Chat2DB contact channel listed by the
[OtterMind organization](https://github.com/OtterMind). Do not open a public
Issue containing personal or sensitive details.

When possible, include what happened, where it happened, who was involved, and
relevant links or context. Maintainers will review reports as privately and
carefully as possible.

Security vulnerabilities follow a separate private process described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take proportionate action to keep the community healthy and
productive. Actions may include clarifying expectations, editing or removing
inappropriate content, asking someone to stop specific behavior, or temporarily
or permanently limiting participation in project spaces.

Enforcement decisions are made by project maintainers based on context and
impact.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies when participating in project spaces and when
representing the project in public community interactions.
